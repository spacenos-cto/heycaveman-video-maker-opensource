# HeyCaveman: Stone Age

Type a lesson, get a cartoon explainer video, in your browser, offline and free, with nothing leaving your device. It's the free, open-source edition of HeyCaveman.

## The Unique Kernel
**The same caveman, acting across every scene, made from your lesson with no AI, no account and no internet.** An offline "director" turns plain text into scenes where your caveman acts out the words: thinking at a question, celebrating a "yay", pointing at steps. Props and captions are matched to what's being said. Set your caveman up once, and he stars in every video.

## Who It's For
- **Main user:** a school teacher who makes a 1–3 minute video to explain photosynthesis, a history event or a grammar rule, then projects it in class or shares it in the class group. They need it fast, safe, with no account, working on a school Chromebook, and fun enough to hold kids' attention.
- **Also:**
  - Students (10–18) doing "explain a concept as a video" projects.
  - Tutors and homeschool parents making a video per topic.
  - NGO and rural trainers with little internet.
  - Hobby creators who find it on GitHub.
- **Today they use** Canva, Animaker or Vyond (monthly fees, and they drag every element themselves), or film themselves, or pay a freelancer.

## The Core Loop
1. **Write:** type or paste a lesson, or build it from a topic or template.
2. **Make:** one button. The first time, pick your caveman.
3. **Watch and tweak:** play it, tap a scene to change it.
4. **Get it:** download the MP4 or save the project file.

They come back for the next lesson: same caveman, same speed, still free.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Look:** flat doodle (bold black outline, flat colours); the caveman acts with clear motions and expressions.
- **Feel:** calm, classroom-safe, kid-appropriate. No pop-ups, no ads, no sign-up, no hype.
- **Experience:** the first thing a person can do is type, and one button gets you to a video.
- **Trust is the brand:** "No account. No tracking. Your lessons never leave your device."
- **Business role:** a trust-first funnel. People who love the free caveman become leads for HeyCaveman premium (AI writing, art packs, more mascots and looks). The upsell is honest and can be hidden entirely for classrooms.
- **UI references** (learner's favourites): https://otterday.app, Bloom, https://forres.tech

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Their kid watches fun, educational animated videos. They want anyone, especially teachers and schools, to make them for almost nothing, "to explore their creativity and curiosity." Stone Age has zero running cost, can pass a school IT review, and earns reach and credibility that fund HeyCaveman.

## What "Working" Looks Like
- A teacher opens the site, taps **Try an example** (or pastes their lesson) and presses **Make my video**.
- They pick their caveman once, and seconds later the video plays: the same caveman acting through every scene, with matching props and big captions.
- They download an MP4. Then they switch Wi-Fi off, reload, and do it all again offline.
- **The "oh, that's cool" beat:** pressing one button turns a plain paragraph into a cartoon where the caveman acts out the lesson, with nothing leaving the device.

## The Scope (built in this order; each milestone ends finished and tested)
1. **M1 Cut:** runs fully on the device. Start screen, Write, Make, Watch, MP4 export (no voice), My videos. Works offline end to end.
2. **M2 Teach:** Lesson Builder, 6 templates, caveman setup, quiz, recap and title cards, gentle sound effects, Classroom mode.
3. **M3 Voice:** built-in voice download, record-my-voice, mixed voices, "voice out of date".
4. **M4 Polish and open:** full Editor, upload your own pictures, accessibility pass, kid-safe art review, help, the For-schools sheet, licence, README.
5. **M5 Bridge:** "Open in HeyCaveman" handoff, a launch discount code, a More looks shelf.

If time runs short, cut from the bottom.

## Later
Nothing deferred within Stone Age; the build order is the safety net.

## Explicitly Cut
- **AI writing, YouTube-link videos, Storybook and other looks, ball and blob mascots, multiple characters, cloud saving:** these are HeyCaveman premium, the upgrade reasons. They never appear in this repo.
- **Photo face on the character:** children's privacy.
- **Teacher dashboards, rosters, assignments:** a possible future premium feature.
- **Languages other than English** for the interface: text stays translatable for later.
- **Phones as a first-class device:** best-effort only.
- **Sharing links or hosting videos:** Stone Age makes a file; people share it however they like.
