The drawings in `public/assets/`, the sound effects in `public/sfx/` and the music in `public/music/` are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Schools, teachers and anyone else can copy, change and reuse them for any purpose, without asking and without credit.

## Where they come from (pre-existing work, disclosed)

- These files were **not made from scratch in this repository**. They are copied from the author's earlier HeyCaveman project, where they were generated by code written with AI assistance (Claude):
  - the drawings are simple SVG shapes;
  - the sounds and music are synthesized from math (no samples or recordings).
- They were created between **5 and 13 October 2026**.
- **No code from that earlier project is used here**; only these output files.
- The tag list that matches lesson words to drawings (`assets/manifest.json`) is new work for this repository.
- Four drawings were left out after a classroom-suitability review (romance, religious or astrology themes), following the product's child-safety rules.

## Added in this repository

- Six drawings were made new for this repository on **22 October 2026**, with AI assistance (Claude), in the same simple SVG style: `sun.svg`, `leaf.svg`, `cloud.svg`, `rain.svg`, `tree.svg` and `water-drop.svg`. They are CC0 like the rest.
